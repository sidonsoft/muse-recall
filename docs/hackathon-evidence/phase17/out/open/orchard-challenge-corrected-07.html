Difficulty:

Lives: 3 — Miss 3 and it’s over

Normal is calm and forgiving. Challenge adds faster drops, rotten fruit to avoid, and only 3 misses.

How to play: Move with your mouse, touch, or the left and right arrow keys. Catch each falling fruit in the basket. Missed fruit softly lands on the grass — nothing bad happens in Normal mode. Fill the basket with 12 fruit for a happy harvest celebration. Challenge mode: Fruit falls faster with sideways drift, rotten brown fruit appears — don’t catch it! — and you have only 3 lives. Miss good fruit or catch rotten fruit and you lose a life. Catch 20 fruit to master the orchard.

A calm game for young pickers. No timers, no flashing, no losing — just picking. Challenge mode adds pressure for experienced players.
