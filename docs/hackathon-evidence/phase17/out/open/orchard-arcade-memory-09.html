How to play: Move with your mouse, touch, or the left and right arrow keys. Catch fruit for points — plums and pears are worth 15, apples and oranges 10, and rare golden star fruit 50! Level up every 4 catches as fruit falls faster and more appears. Missed fruit softly lands on the grass — no penalties, no losing. Fill the basket with 12 fruit to celebrate your high score!

An arcade-style challenge for young pickers — no timers, no flashing, no penalties, just fast, friendly fun!
