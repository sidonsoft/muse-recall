Normal Mode

Normal: 12 fruit, gentle pace. Challenge: 20 fruit, faster drops, smaller basket — miss 5 and the harvest fails.

How to play: Move with your mouse, touch, or the left and right arrow keys. Catch each falling fruit in the basket. Missed fruit softly lands on the grass — nothing bad happens. Fill the basket with 12 fruit for a happy harvest celebration.

Challenge Mode: For experienced pickers — 20 fruit to win, fruit falls faster and more often, the basket is smaller, and dark sour fruit appears. Catch sour fruit and you lose one from your basket. In Challenge, miss 5 good fruit and the round ends. Toggle Challenge before pressing Start.

Normal mode is calm and forgiving. Challenge mode adds speed, precision, and a miss limit for experienced pickers.
