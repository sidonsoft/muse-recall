How to play: Move with your mouse, touch, or the left and right arrow keys (A/D also work). Catch falling fruit for points — plum and pear are worth more, golden star fruit is a rare bonus! Build a combo by catching quickly for extra points. Missed fruit lands softly on the grass — nothing bad happens. Each level needs a few more fruit and the orchard gets a little faster. No timers, no flashing, just cheerful arcade picking!
