Normal: calm pace, 12 fruit to fill the basket. Challenge: faster drops, smaller basket, 20 fruit to win — plus rotten fruit that costs a point if caught.

How to play: Move with your mouse, touch, or the left and right arrow keys. Catch each falling fruit in the basket. Missed fruit softly lands on the grass — nothing bad happens. Fill the basket with 12 fruit for a happy harvest celebration. Challenge Mode is for experienced players: fruit falls faster and drifts, the basket is smaller, you need 20 fruit, and you must avoid rotten fruit.

A calm game for young pickers. No timers, no flashing, no losing — just picking.

Challenge Mode adds speed, a smaller basket, and rotten fruit to dodge — for players who want a harder harvest.
