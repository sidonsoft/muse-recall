Orchard Catch

Move the basket left and right to catch soft, ripe fruit falling from the trees. Fill the basket to finish picking!

Difficulty:

Caught 0 of 12 fruit

Press Start to begin picking.

How to play: Move with your mouse, touch, or the left and right arrow keys. Catch each falling fruit in the basket. Missed fruit softly lands on the grass — nothing bad happens. Fill the basket with 12 fruit for a happy harvest celebration. Hard Challenge: Faster fruit, smaller basket, 20 to win — miss 5 and the harvest is over. Avoid the dark sour fruit!

A calm orchard for focused pickers. Try Normal for a relaxed harvest, or Hard Challenge for a fast, unforgiving test.
