How to play: Move with your mouse, touch, or the left and right arrow keys. Catch each falling fruit in the basket — each fruit adds points! Every 3 catches you reach a new level and fruit falls a little faster for a gentle arcade challenge. Missed fruit softly lands on the grass — nothing bad happens. Fill the basket with 12 fruit for a happy harvest celebration.

A calm game for young pickers. No timers, no flashing, no losing — just picking.
