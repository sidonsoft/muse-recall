How to play: Move with your mouse, touch, or the left and right arrow keys. Catch each falling fruit in the basket. Missed fruit softly lands on the grass — nothing bad happens. Look for shiny golden apples for bonus points! Every 10 fruit you level up and the orchard gets a little faster. Fill the basket with 30 fruit for a happy harvest celebration.

A calm game for young pickers. No timers, no flashing, no losing — just picking.
