How to play: Move with your mouse, touch, or the left and right arrow keys. Catch falling fruit for 10 points — chain catches without missing to build a combo bonus (up to +10). Golden fruit is worth 50! Rotten fruit costs a life if you catch it, but dodging one earns +5. Missed good fruit costs a life. You have 3 lives, and the fruit falls faster every 4 catches.
