How to play: Move with your mouse, touch, or the left and right arrow keys. Catch ripe fruit for 10 points (streaks earn bonus points) and golden fruit for 50 points and 2 basket fills. Dodge the dark sour fruit — catching one costs a life. Missing a good fruit costs a life too. You have 3 lives; fruit falls faster every 4 catches. Fill the basket with 12 fruit to win!
