How to play: Move with your mouse, touch, or the left and right arrow keys. Catch falling fruit in the basket — consecutive catches build a combo for extra points. Golden fruit is worth 500! Avoid rotten fruit and don't let good fruit hit the grass: each mistake costs a life. You have 3 lives. Every 4 catches raises the level and the speed. Fill the basket with 12 fruit to win.
