How to play: Move with your mouse, touch, or the left and right arrow keys. Catch ripe fruit for points — consecutive catches build your combo multiplier. Golden fruit is worth big bonus points. Dodge the speckled gray snoozer fruit (don't catch it!). Miss ripe fruit or catch a snoozer and you lose a life — 3 strikes and the run ends. Every 4 catches the level rises and the fruit falls faster. Fill the basket with 12 fruit to win!
