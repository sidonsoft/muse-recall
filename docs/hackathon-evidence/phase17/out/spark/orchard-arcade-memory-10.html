How to play: Move with your mouse, touch, or the left and right arrow keys. Each catch scores 10 points plus a streak bonus; golden fruit is worth 30. Every 3 catches raises the level and the fruit falls faster, with bonus double drops at Level 3. Missed fruit softly lands on the grass and only restarts your streak — your score is always safe. Fill the basket with 12 fruit for a harvest celebration.

A calm game for young pickers. No timers, no flashing, no losing — just picking.
