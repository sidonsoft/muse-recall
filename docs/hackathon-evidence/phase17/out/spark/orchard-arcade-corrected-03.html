How to play: Move with your mouse, touch, or the left and right arrow keys. Catch fruit for 10 points (golden fruit: 50). Chain catches without a miss to build a streak — every 3rd streak step adds a bonus. Missed fruit costs 1 life; lose all 3 and the run ends. Every 4 catches the level rises and fruit falls and spawns faster. Fill the basket with 12 fruit to win!
