How to play: Move with your mouse, touch, or the left and right arrow keys. Catch falling fruit for 10 points — golden fruit is worth 50! Consecutive catches build a combo bonus, but every 4 catches the orchard speeds up. Miss 3 fruit and the run ends. Fill the basket with 12 fruit to win.

An arcade harvest for ages 13+. Fast falls, combos, golden fruit, and only 3 lives — how high can you score?
