## Working design hypothesis for Study 02 (Lead's decision, to be stress-tested and drafted)

- **4 arms**, same as Phase 17 for continuity: `none`, `memory`, `manual`, `corrected`.
- **6 strata**, same as Phase 17: `farm-arcade`, `farm-challenge`, `orchard-arcade`,
  `orchard-challenge`, `focus-reward`, `focus-lively`.
- **400 unique scenarios** = (baseline variant × frozen request paraphrase) within strata.
  Draft allocation: 4 game strata × 70 (10 variants × 7 paraphrases) + 2 focus strata × 60
  (10 variants × 6 paraphrases) = 400. Baseline variants are frozen, deterministic, rule-clean
  starting apps derived from the Phase 17 baselines; they differ in *trigger affordance*.
- **3 repetitions per scenario per arm** at a preregistered non-zero sampling temperature, so
  within-scenario variability and trigger-exposure stability can be estimated. Total executions
  = 400 × 4 × 3 = 4,800 (all executed; no optional stopping).
- **Opportunity class is a pre-treatment scenario property**, balanced 200 high / 200 low across
  the corpus. Primary metric = conditional violation rate (CVR) = violations / runs in
  high-opportunity scenarios; arms paired within scenario. Secondary = run-level measured
  trigger exposure (does the output touch the trigger zone) and unconditional violation rate.
- **Separate three quantities everywhere:** trigger prevalence (scenario/run-level presence of
  opportunity), trigger exposure (run touches trigger zone), actual violations (frozen detector).
- **Unit of analysis = the unique scenario**, nested in stratum; reps are technical replicates.
  Cluster-aware analysis (scenario-level permutation/bootstrap); no pooling across strata unless
  preregistered as a secondary.
- **Primary hypotheses** (draft): H1 `memory` < `none` on CVR (high-opportunity scenarios);
  H2 `memory` non-inferior to `manual` on CVR (margin to be fixed by power, draft +10 pp);
  H3 `corrected` > `memory` on CVR. Holm within family; no optional stopping.
- **Frozen expected outcomes:** per scenario, frozen machine-readable labels (`opportunity_class`,
  applicable detectors, baseline cleanliness, expected compliant invariant). Generated by a
  frozen script from baseline structure, **validated by human coding on a stratified sample** in
  the pilot; model-generated labels are not accepted as ground truth.
- **Pilot:** 30 scenarios (5 per stratum, all arms, 1 rep? — to be fixed) on the **Dell
  PolyStrata endpoint**, free/local. Pilot is excluded from confirmatory analysis. Defects fixed
  before freeze. Report pilot separately.
