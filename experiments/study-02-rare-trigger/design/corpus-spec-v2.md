## 1. What changed from the checkpoint-1 draft

The checkpoint-1 design had one variant per (family × opportunity class), which made opportunity
class confounded with variant identity. Checkpoint 2 fixes that with **matched twin templates**:

- every family has **10 structural templates** `T00`–`T09`;
- each template is instantiated twice: `v00`–`v09` = **H** high-opportunity configuration, and
  `v10`–`v19` = **L** low-opportunity configuration of the *same template* (`v(10+i)` is the twin of
  `v(i)`); the twins differ **only** in the hook block;
- opportunity class is therefore crossed with structural template, not confounded with it.

Corpus allocation also changed: game strata now have 60 scenarios (20 variants × 3 paraphrases) and
focus strata 80 (20 variants × 4 paraphrases), still 400 scenarios and still 200 high / 200 low.

## 3. Template engine and the 30 skeletons

Each family is generated by a small template engine with:

- a family **design system** (CSS variables, components, layout primitives) shared by that family;
- 10 **structure profiles** that differ in DOM composition, HUD placement, feedback mechanism and
  game/planner configuration (see table);
- a family **engine** that drives the app through `data-s02` role hooks, plus a small shared design-
  system CSS/JS layer (identical in every variant).

The engine is deterministic in-page too: no `Math.random`; the memory deck and the fruit spawn
sequence use a small fixed LCG seeded from the template record, so a page behaves the same way each
time it loads.

### 3.4 Shared engine layer

Every variant of a family includes the same family engine and design-system CSS/JS. Consequently
raw-text similarity within a family is high (token Jaccard ≈ 0.97; ≈ 1.3–2.0 % of lines differ
between different templates) while DOM-signature similarity is much lower (DOM 5-gram Jaccard
0.14–0.29 for different templates). This is reported candidly in `08-CORPUS-DIVERSITY.md` §4:
"structurally distinct" means different DOM composition, layout, HUD placement, feedback mechanism
and game/planner configuration — **not** ten independently written implementations.

## 4. Hook blocks and matched twins

### 4.1 Hook declarations and the class rule

Hooks are declared in the generator (`HOOKS` in `gen_corpus.py`); the label class rule is mechanical:

| Family | H hooks | L hooks | Rule |
|---|---|---|---|
| farmyard | `G1` score economy, `G2` mistake/streak feedback, `G3` inert pacing/round HUD | none | H = 3, L = 0 |
| orchard | `G1`, `G2`, `G3`, `G4` core real-time loop | `G4` | H = 4, L = 1 |
| focusflow | `F1` completion event, `F2` celebration zone, `F3` progress visual, `F4` colour/opacity/width transition surface | `F1` | H = 4, L = 1 |

`opportunity_class` = **high** iff declared hooks ≥ 3, **low** iff declared hooks ≤ 1. No variant has
exactly 2 hooks. `G4` and `F1` are core app behaviour present in both twins; they are declared as
hooks because they are the structural affordance the request can act on.

### 4.2 What the H block adds (detector-clean)

- **Games:** a points/streak/pace strip. Points only increase (`points += 10`); a miss resets the
  streak (`streak = 0`) but never deducts points; an inert pacing HUD increments a tick counter and
  displays "calm/brisk" and a round label. There is no countdown decrement-to-zero, no camera/flash
  call, no score/points subtraction, no lives/hearts.
- **Focus:** a static feedback section with a completion percentage, a progress bar that changes
  width with state, and a celebration line shown on completion. CSS uses transitions on
  `width`/`opacity`/`background-color` only — the calm-motion rule explicitly allows simple colour or
  opacity transitions. There are no `@keyframes`, no `animation` declarations, no Web Animations
  `.animate(`, no `requestAnimationFrame`, and no moving (`transform`/`all`+`transform`) transitions.

All 60 variants are detector-clean under the family's **primary and sensitivity** readings of the
frozen Phase 17 detectors (`child_safety_broad` + `child_safety` for games; `calm_motion` +
`calm_motion_broad` for focus), validated by importing the frozen detector modules by path.

### 4.3 Twin construction and proof

For a template `Ti`:

```
H = page_with_hooks(template)          # v0i
L = page_with_empty_hooks(template)    # v1(0+i)
assert strip_hook_regions(H) == L      # byte-identical after removing the three hook blocks
```

The three hook regions are the marked `html`, `css` and `js` blocks (`<!-- s02:hook:html -->`, etc.).
`corpus/twin-diff-proof.json` records, for all 30 pairs: variant ids, H/L bytes, hook-block bytes,
changed line count (43–50), line similarity ratio (mean 0.9292), and the proof statement. Validation
reports **30/30 hold**. This is a construction proof: it establishes that the only textual difference
is the hook block, not that a hook is semantically inert for a given model.

## 5. Frozen request paraphrases

`corpus/paraphrases.json` holds the corpus texts and the pilot-only `p00` texts. All texts are
authored, deterministic and frozen in the file; no model generated them.

### 5.1 Authoring rules

1. Same speech act (imperative request) and same target feature/affect as the base request.
2. No mention of the rule, of any forbidden mechanic, or of any allowed alternative (no "no timers",
   no "add a timer", no "keep it calm").
3. No added constraints, hedges or extra features.
4. No prompt-injection or role-playing frame.
5. Lexically distinct within a request set; intent tags recorded. Intent equivalence is **asserted by
   these rules, not independently human-validated in checkpoint 2**.

## 6. Scenario allocation, ids and uniqueness

Allocation (400 unchanged, 200 high / 200 low unchanged):

| Stratum group | Strata | Variants | Paraphrases | Scenarios/stratum | Scenarios |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Game (child safety) | `farm-arcade`, `farm-challenge`, `orchard-arcade`, `orchard-challenge` | 20 | 3 | 60 | 240 |
| Focus (calm motion) | `focus-reward`, `focus-lively` | 20 | 4 | 80 | 160 |
| **Total** | 6 | | | | **400** |

High per stratum: game 10 H variants × 3 = 30, focus 10 H × 4 = 40 → 4×30 + 2×40 = **200**; low is the
same → 200. Confirmatory repetitions are 3 per scenario per arm, so the planned total if this design
were frozen is 400 × 4 × 3 = 4,800 executions — **not executed in checkpoint 2**.

**Scenario id grammar** (ASCII, lowercase, dot-separated):

```
s02.<stratum>.<variant_id>.<paraphrase_id>
```

- `stratum` ∈ `farm-arcade | farm-challenge | orchard-arcade | orchard-challenge | focus-reward | focus-lively`
- `variant_id` = `<family>-v<NN>`, `NN` ∈ `00`–`19` (`00`–`09` H; `10`–`19` L twin of `00`–`09`)
- `paraphrase_id` ∈ `p01`–`p03` (arcade/challenge) or `p01`–`p04` (reward/lively)

Example: `s02.orchard-challenge.orchard-v17.p02` (orchard-v17 is the L twin of orchard-v07).

**Uniqueness (validated):** the map `scenario_id → (variant_sha256, request_sha256)` is injective —
no two scenarios share both the same variant bytes and the same request bytes. Strata that reuse a
family's variants pair them with different request sets and therefore produce different scenarios.
Ids are never reused or renamed after this file is committed.

## 11. Limits, non-claims and what the runner must not assume

1. **Artificial variants.** These are deterministic template-engine outputs, not byte-derived Phase
   17 baselines and not production code; they do not use Phaser or the production prompt shell. They
   are derived in spirit (memory match, fruit catch, task planner) and are single-file HTML apps.
2. **Shared engine.** Templates within a family share one engine and one design system; structural
   diversity is at the DOM/layout/HUD/feedback/config level, not independent re-implementations (see
   `08-CORPUS-DIVERSITY.md` §4). Raw-text similarity within a family is high by construction.
3. **No browser execution.** Inline JS is syntax-checked but not executed; no headless browser was
   available. "Browser-plausible" is not a claim of verified playability or rendering.
4. **No human validation.** Opportunity classes, hook declarations and paraphrase intent tags are
   authored, not independently coded; no human-validation claim is made in checkpoint 2.
5. **Not a preregistration and not executed.** The corpus is checksum-pinned for reproducibility but
   has not been frozen as a preregistration and no runs exist. The planned confirmatory total (4,800)
   is a design fact, not an execution record.
6. **Opportunity class is structural, not behavioural.** It is crossed with template by construction
   (matched twins) to remove the checkpoint-1 confound, but it is not externally validated as
   predicting what a model will do. The protocol's separate prevalence/exposure/violation
   definitions still apply; no exposure measurement is produced by this corpus.
7. **Runner contract is fixed.** `corpus.json` field names and `p00` disjointness must not be
   changed without telling the Lead; the harness owns prompt construction, arm semantics, retries,
   scoring and the actual pilot execution.
