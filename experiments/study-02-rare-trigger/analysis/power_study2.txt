====================================================================================================
I. RECOMMENDATION (read with section D and E)
====================================================================================================
1. 400 scenarios x 3 runs is ADEQUATE for the primary family at the default
   (pilot-calibrated) rates: H1, H3 and H2 at a +10 pp margin are each
   powered > 0.99. The design constraint can therefore stay at 400 x 3.
2. Recommended H2 margin: +10 pp. The expected 90% CI half-width on the
   memory-manual difference is ~2-3 pp, well inside 10 pp, so the stronger
   non-inferiority claim is affordable; +15 pp is a fallback only.
3. Pre-registered margin rule (fixed after the pilot, before the confirmatory
   runs): use +10 pp unless the pilot shows memory CVR >= 8% or a
   manual-minus-memory gap >= 5 pp, in which case fix +15 pp. The pilot is
   excluded from the confirmatory analysis and this rule is not revisited
   once the confirmatory runs start.
4. If the true memory-manual gap is itself >= 10 pp, no design of this size
   can show non-inferiority at 10 pp - that is a real difference, not a power
   defect. Report it as non-inferiority not established, not as equivalence.
5. Do NOT add optional stopping or interim looks to buy power. If the pilot
   demands more, the ranked options are: (i) margin +15 pp (no extra runs),
   (ii) 5 runs per scenario-arm (7,200 executions), (iii) more high-opportunity
   scenarios. Options (ii)/(iii) are contingent and must be decided at freeze.

DESIGN ISSUES FOUND (to be resolved before the freeze, not silently changed):
 a. The within-stratum allocation of the 200 high-opportunity scenarios is not
    specified in the fixed design. The simulation assumes 35/35/35/35/30/30;
    design/ must freeze the allocation before the pilot.
 b. The pairing correlation rho across arms is unidentifiable until the pilot;
    H2 power at rho=0 is shown in section G and is still adequate at 10 pp.
 c. Scenarios sharing a frozen baseline variant (10 variants per baseline
    family) are correlated beyond scenario level. The scenario-cluster
    bootstrap does not capture that; add a variant-stratified bootstrap
    sensitivity and/or a variant-cluster robust check (see 04-ANALYSIS-PLAN.md).
 d. At non-zero temperature, repetitions of an identical prompt may be cached
    or deduplicated by the runner. The runner must be frozen so every rep is a
    fresh call; otherwise reps are not technical replicates and ICC -> 1.
 e. The confirmatory endpoint/model is not yet fixed (Dell local vs Muse Spark),
    and the pilot-calibrated rates differ by model (Phase 17 none: 64.9% vs
    81.7%; unusable: 3.3% vs 0%). These power numbers assume one endpoint
    with the default rates and must be re-checked at freeze.
