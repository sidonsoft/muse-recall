C2. T6 CORRECTION - FAMILY-BLOCK VARIANT-CLUSTER BOOTSTRAP
========================================================================================================
The checkpoint-2 plan originally resampled the 10 H-variant clusters WITHIN
each stratum independently. That is anti-conservative: every H variant appears
in BOTH strata of its family (same variant bytes; 3 game / 4 focus scenarios per
stratum), so the two stratum means of a family covary through the shared
variants. For a variant, the cross-stratum correlation of its two stratum means
is c ~= rho_V*ICC / (rho_V*ICC + (1-rho_V*ICC)/k), k = 3 or 4; with 6 equal-
weight strata and 3 family-shared pairs the variance is understated by ~(1+c).
At the default assumptions (rho_V = PHI + (1-PHI)*rho_pair = 0.65, rho_V*ICC =
0.1625, c = 0.37 game / 0.44 focus, mean ~0.39) the factor is ~1.39 in variance
(SE x1.18). The simulation below re-derives c and the design effect.

PRIMARY (corrected): resample the 10 H-variant template clusters of each
family AS A BLOCK (both strata kept together) and equal-weight the 3 families:
    Delta* = (1/3) sum_f (1/2)(mean_{g1}* + mean_{g2}*)
Normal approximation: Var(Delta) = (1/9) sum_f (1/n_f) s^2(z_f),
    z_{f,v} = (c_{v,g1}+c_{v,g2})/2,  c = variant cluster mean difference.
SENSITIVITY (pre-declared, anti-conservative): the old per-stratum independent
scheme, reported beside the primary to quantify the correction.
The generative model already contains the cross-stratum correlation (the H
variant effect is shared by both strata), so only the estimator changes.

K2. MARGIN: FROZEN +10 pp (D4 accepted; T8 single fallback rule).
  Frozen margin: +10 pp. Pre-declared fallback (fixed at freeze; never revisited
  once confirmatory data exist): fix +15 pp instead if the pilot's usable
  high-opportunity primary runs contain at least 3 memory-arm violations and at
  most 1 manual-arm violation; otherwise the margin stays +10 pp. No other pilot
  quantity may move the margin. (Pilot observed: memory 0/16, manual 0/15 - the
  fallback does not trigger.)
  Why +10 pp: with the T6 correction the arm-parity grid minimum is 0.834 at 10 pp,
  0.693 at 8 pp (8 pp is not defensible) and 0.985 at 15 pp
  (15 pp is needlessly weak when no degradation is expected). The small-deficit
  region is disclosed: at a true +5 pp deficit, H2@10 min power is 0.485
  (H2@15 0.864); that is a real-difference region, not an N defect.

K3. ADEQUACY: keep 400 x 3. H1/H3 are saturated (1.000). With the T6
  family-block correction, H2@10 at arm parity has grid minimum 0.834
  (T6 moves the default cell from the old 1.38 DE / 5.03 pp CI to the family-block
  1.91 DE / 5.89 pp CI), so the design is
  adequate at the frozen margin. It is NOT adequate to guarantee that a true
  +5 pp memory deficit is called non-inferior at +10 pp under strong clustering
  (min 0.485); that is disclosed, not compensated by optional stopping.
  The scenario-level bootstrap of v1 and the old per-stratum scheme are both
  anti-conservative for H2 and are never reported as the confirmatory interval.

K4. DESIGN WEAKNESSES / CORRECTIONS (for the Lead):
 a. T6: the original per-stratum variant bootstrap broke the cross-stratum
    correlation of a variant and understated the H2 variance by ~(1+c) ~1.4x. It
    is replaced by the family-block bootstrap (primary); the old scheme is kept
    only as a pre-declared sensitivity. All H2/CI numbers here are T6-corrected.
 b. T8: the margin fallback is now one denominator-free rule (K2); the earlier
    inconsistent 3/18-vs-3/15 and manual<=6%-vs-5% variants are removed.
 c. Template ICC and matched-pair correlation are not independent random-effect
    levels in this corpus: a template contains exactly its H/L twin pair, so the
    two knobs combine into ONE pair-level correlation. The grid maps to 9 distinct
    values (section B); report it that way rather than as a 3x3 cross.
 d. For the high-opportunity-only primary analysis, template clustering is not a
    separate level from variant clustering (one H twin per template). The separate
    template design effect exists only for full-corpus/secondary quantities.
 e. 10 variant clusters per family makes percentile intervals coarse; the point
    estimate and the interval width should be read together near the margin.
 f. The revised allocation trades request diversity for cluster count: game strata
    have 30 high-op scenarios (was 35) and 3 paraphrases per variant (was 7);
    focus strata have 40 (was 30) and 4 (was 6). See section F.
 g. The matched-twin mechanism does not improve H1/H2/H3 (they use high-op only);
    it buys the opportunity-class confound fix and the matched H-vs-L secondary.
 h. rho_arm and PHI remain unidentifiable pre-pilot; sections G/G3 bound them.
